# What's working

Written every Monday by the weekly performance report. The daily posting run reads this file and leans into what's working. Takeaways only, no raw numbers (this repo is public).

## Week of Nov 9–15, 2026

Audience is still tiny, so treat these as early signals, not rules.

**Do more of**
- PM slot (15:00 PT). Afternoon posts out-reached morning posts this week.
- Friday and Saturday. Weekend posts did clearly better than Tue–Thu.
- Myth-vs-fact hooks ("Myth or fact: ..."). The freezer myth post was the week's best and the only scheduled post to get a share and a click.
- Concrete, everyday-object app posts (store cards / Wallet did well). Name one specific annoying moment, then the feature.
- Timely Thanksgiving angles this week: leftovers, the big shop, shared family list.
- Questions that can be answered in one word or a number (lower effort than "tell us your story").

**Do less of**
- Mid-week abstract feature posts (Meals planning) and long stat-heavy captions. Both drew nothing.
- Open-ended "share in the comments" asks on every post. No one is commenting yet; vary the CTA (save this, tag someone, follow for launch).
- The identical hashtag block on every post. Rotate in niche, timely tags (e.g. Thanksgiving leftovers, meal prep, pantry organization).

**Best slot:** PM, Fri/Sat.
**Best pillar:** attention-grabbing hook (slight edge over food-waste fact; app posts close behind). Paper image style edged cyan, but the gap is small.
